## Candidate 1 — SELECTED (139 characters)

Why it wins: the roast is an arithmetic proof, not an insult — the refusal cost more words than the task would have, which makes the agent's failure measurable. Anyone who has used an agent has received that 900-word apology, and the phrase 'word for word' turns recognition into the punchline.
